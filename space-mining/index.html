Space material mining

A company that mines basic materials like silicon and titanium in space, to build things there.

Follow the build Test the economics

The problem

Everything built in orbit or on the Moon is launched from Earth, and launch costs thousands of dollars per kilogram to orbit and far more to the lunar surface. Every habitat, solar array and factory pays that tax on every kilogram of raw material.

The idea

Make the raw materials where they are used. The lunar surface is rich in silicon, aluminium, iron and oxygen, and titanium-bearing ilmenite is found in the Moon's dark maria. Mining and refining it locally turns the most expensive part of building in space into a local supply chain.

Roadmap

Phase 0 · Now
Validate demand and the numbers. Talk to in-space manufacturing, lunar-base and satellite-servicing companies about what materials they would buy, and in what form.
Phase 1 · Ground
Prototype regolith processing (simulant) on Earth: extraction of oxygen, silicon and metal, and sintering or 3D printing of bricks.
Phase 2 · Demo
Ride-share a small experiment on a commercial lunar lander or orbital test mission.
Phase 3 · Operate
Sell processed material (oxygen, metal feedstock, construction blocks) to builders on the Moon and in orbit.

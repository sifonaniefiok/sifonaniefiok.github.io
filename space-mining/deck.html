Pitch deck

Nine slides. Scroll.

01

Problem

Building in space costs thousands of dollars per kilogram to orbit and far more to the Moon, because every kilogram is launched from Earth.

02

Solution

Mine and refine silicon, titanium, aluminium, iron and oxygen from lunar regolith where it is needed, and sell it to builders in space.

03

Why now

Commercial lunar landers are flying, NASA's Artemis programme and private bases need local supply, and launch costs keep falling.

04

Product

Three lines in order: sintered construction blocks, oxygen, then metals and silicon feedstock.

05

Market

Lunar bases, orbital manufacturing, satellite servicing and space agencies. Early buyers are agencies and prime contractors.

06

Traction / plan

Phase 0 validate demand; Phase 1 ground prototype on simulant; Phase 2 ride-share demo; Phase 3 operate.

07

Competition

AstroForge, Interlune and ispace focus on asteroid metals, helium-3 and landers. Few sell building materials to customers in space.

08

Team

Founder: Sifon Imaikop. Seeking: a materials/chemical engineer, an aerospace engineer and a space-law adviser.

09

The ask

Seed funding and grants to build the ground processing prototype and a first customer pilot. Amount to be set after the Phase 0 numbers.

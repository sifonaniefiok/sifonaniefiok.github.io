Good to know

Driving in North Cyprus

Keep left

Cyprus drives on the left, and roundabouts go clockwise. If you drive in the UK or Ireland, it will feel like home.

What do I need to rent a car?

Your passport and your driving licence. Most visitors can drive on the licence from their home country. You need to be 21 or older and have held your licence for at least two years.

Can I drive to the South?

Yes. Our insurance covers the North only, so buy separate cover for the South at the crossing point before you drive through. It takes a few minutes.

What does the insurance cover?

Full cover with no excess for accidents, theft and damage to the car in North Cyprus. Tyres, glass and damage from off‑road driving are not covered.

What if my flight is late?

We track every flight, so we wait for you at no extra cost. If your plans change, send us a WhatsApp message.

How do I pay?

Pay when you get the car, by cash (pounds, euros or lira) or card. There is no deposit and no hold on your card.

Can I pick up at Larnaca Airport?

Yes, for a £25 delivery charge. We meet you at arrivals in Larnaca and drive you across to the North to start your rental.
